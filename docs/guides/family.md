# The family guide

How to use the family's app, day to day. Short sections; skip to what you need.

Your family gave the app its own name, colors and mascot, and picked what the kids earn:
stickers, stars, points or something of your own. This guide says "the app", "stickers" and
"the mascot"; yours may say something else. Some apps may be switched off for your family, and
then they simply aren't there.

Something not right, or something here doesn't match what you see? Tell an admin (one of the
grown-ups who looks after the app). Whoever runs the server has [the admin guide](admin.md).

## Getting in

### Open the address

An admin will tell you the app's address, something like `https://familyos.example.com`.
Always use that exact address: your passkey only works there.

### Cloudflare's sign-in, about once a month

Before the app itself, a sign-in page from Cloudflare asks who you are: your family Microsoft
account, or a code sent to your email. That keeps everyone outside the family away from the app.
Each phone, tablet or computer does it about once a month. A shared kids' tablet needs a grown-up
to do it.

<!-- screenshot: the Cloudflare Access sign-in page -->

### Add it to your Home Screen

It then opens like any other app, full screen, with the family's icon.

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the address in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
  Open it from the Home Screen and sign in there: it keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari.
- **Android:** in Chrome, open the menu (⋮) and choose **Add to Home screen** (or **Install
  app**).
- **Computer:** most browsers offer to install it from the address bar.

### Grown-ups: your passkey

A passkey is how you sign in: your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN. No password to remember,
and nobody can trick you into typing it somewhere else.

1. An admin sends you a **one-time invite link** (it works once, for 48 hours).
2. Open it **on your own phone**, signed out. The page says **You're invited**.
3. Tap **Make my passkey** and let your phone do its fingerprint, face or PIN. That's it.

Next time, tap **Sign in with a passkey**. If your family uses Microsoft accounts, tap **Sign in
with Microsoft** instead, with your family Microsoft account. (If the invite offers your family's
own sign-in service, "Use … instead", you can use that.)

Add a **spare passkey** on a second device (Settings → My passkeys), so losing your phone doesn't
lock you out.

<!-- screenshot: the invite page with Make my passkey -->

### Who's using the app? The profile picker

After signing in you see everyone's tiles: **Tap your sticker!**

- **Your own tile** opens straight away after you sign in. If you locked it with a PIN (below),
  it asks for the PIN when you come back to it from someone else's profile.
- **Kids' tiles** ask for their PIN (4 to 6 digits).
- **Another grown-up's tile** with their own sign-in only opens for them: tap **Sign out so they
  can sign in**.
- Five wrong PINs in a row lock that tile for a while (15 minutes, longer each time).

To change who's using the app, open your name at the top right and tap **Switch person**.

<!-- screenshot: the profile picker -->

### My look

Your look is yours: nobody else sees it, and it follows you to every device. Open your name at the
top right and tap **My look** (it's in Settings too). Tap one and the app tries it on right
there, over whatever page you're on. Pick a look, then one of its colors. **Keep it** saves it; **Put it back** or Esc leaves things as
they were. The first time you reach Home, the app asks once. Each look has its own colors, and its own way of marking a finished chore. Day or night stays per device, so a tablet in a dark room can stay dark. Looks also add a few small lines of their own beside the app's usual words, just for fun; the usual words still say what everything is. If your device uses a high contrast setting, like Windows contrast themes, the look's art steps aside and its fonts stay.

### Big buttons or Full app

Open your name at the top right. Under "How the app looks for me":

- **Big buttons**: just my stuff, extra large. Made for younger kids: their chores, meds,
  school and stickers.
- **Full app**: everything I can use.

In the same menu you can tell the mascot how much to talk (**Chatty**, **Just cheers** or
**Off**), switch person, or **Sign out**. On a computer, once you've tapped him, he hops along
the bottom of the page after your mouse; tap him and he stands up on his hind legs to say
something. If your device is set to reduce motion, he stays put. A grown-up can choose where each person starts in
Settings → Starting views.

## Finding your way around

- **The rail** (on a computer or tablet) lists the apps down the side: Home, People, Calendar,
  Chores, Meds, Groceries, then **Show more** for the rest.
- **The bottom bar** (on a phone) has the first four, and **All apps** for the rest.
- **The nine dots** at the top open every app at once.
- **Search** at the top finds people, chores, groceries and more.
- **The bell** shows your notifications: orders, notes from grown-ups, sign-in changes. Opening
  it marks the ones it shows as read; if there are more, the count stays. The Notifications page
  keeps the latest 100, and anything new there counts as read once you've seen it.
- The theme switch at the top cycles through matching your device, light and dark.
- **On a keyboard,** the first Tab shows **Skip to content**, which jumps past the top bar and the
  rail.

Kids see only the apps meant for them. Money, Cars, the Garage, Data and Settings are for
grown-ups.

## Home

Your day at a glance. **Today** comes first: chores and meds to tick off right there, with what's
finished folded under **✓ done** (Undo is in there). Then **This week's calendar**, **School this
week**, **Upcoming dates** (birthdays and anniversaries), **Vehicle maintenance**, and **Recent
activity**.

**Haven't talked in a while** lists people you haven't been in touch with lately, with a quick
📞 or 💬 to log it ("Just called", "Just texted"). Teachers, school offices and a med's doctor or
pharmacy aren't on it; for anyone else you don't want reminders about (the plumber), set their
**Check-in reminder** to 0.

Signed in with Microsoft? Your own Outlook calendar shows on your own profile. Pick which
calendar in Settings → Calendar on Home.

In **Big buttons** view, Home is just **My chores**, **My meds today**, **School this week** and a
peek at the store.

## People

The family's address book: everyone in the house, plus grandparents, friends, teachers, doctors.

- **+ Add person**, or search by name, email or phone. A full name works too ("Ivy Parker").
- Each person's page has their **Key information**, important dates, and **Recent
  interactions**: log a call, visit or note in the box at the top (**Log it**), and change the
  date if it happened earlier.
- **Around the house** shows a family member's chores, stickers, list items and school.
- Grown-ups can mark a note "grown-ups only" so kids don't see it.
- With Microsoft, a contact's page can show **Recent emails** and **Sync from Microsoft** fills in
  their photo, name and phone.
- Household members show what's open at a glance: **2 overdue**, **1 missing**, **Test Thu**
  (kids see only their own).
- A family member's page has **Open items**: their chores for the week, their homework and tests,
  and forms they're handling, each with its status to tap. Tap an item to see it on the side, with
  its history. Kids looking at a brother's or sister's page see their chores only, as plain text.
  In Big buttons the page keeps its simple summary.

## Calendar

The family's whole week in one place: chores and meds, birthdays and dates, homework and school
calendars, car maintenance, and your own Outlook events (on your own profile, if you sign in with
Microsoft). Tap anything to go where it lives. The arrows and **Today** move around, and **Month** shows the
whole month's events (chores and doses stay on the weeks). Grown-ups can pick one person with the
name chips: each row's color shows whose it is (two people sharing a first name get their own chip). A day's doses share one row; tap it to see them.

Kids see the family calendar too, with their own school things (no cars, no Outlook).

An admin can also keep a shared calendar in Outlook filled with birthdays and car due dates
(**Outlook sync ⚙️**).

## Chores and stickers

The chores board shows what's **due today**, what's **late**, and what's coming up.

- Tick a chore off when it's done (**Done** on the board and Home, **Done!** in Big
  buttons). Made a mistake? Tap the undo arrow. Kids tick their own chores and Anyone chores.
- **Anyone: grab it!** chores are for whoever gets there first; kids can grab them too.
- What's after today is folded under **Next 14 days**. Tap it to look ahead.
- Grown-ups can **Skip** a chore from its label (it's called **Missed** once it's overdue: nobody
  gets stickers for it), and **Give to** someone else from its **⋯**. The **⋯** also has **Move to
  tomorrow** for a chore due today or overdue, and **Move to today** for an overdue one, when that
  day is free; **Move back** puts it where it was (a chore moved to tomorrow is under **Next 14
  days**, so its Move back is there too). Daily chores don't move (tomorrow's is already
  there): skip one instead. Chores from before last week that were never
  marked can be cleared with **Mark them missed**.
- Each chore shows where it stands: **Due today**, **Overdue**, **To do**, **Done**, **Skipped** or
  **Missed**. When you can change it, the label has a little arrow: tap it to pick what's next
  (**Done**, **Skip**, **Undo**). Not in Big buttons.
- **Grown-ups: change several at once.** Tap **Select**, tick the chores, and use the bar at the
  bottom: **Done**, **Skip**, **Move to tomorrow** or **Give to…** (up to 30 at a time). It says
  what it did and what it skipped, and why (already done, or a day that's taken). With one person
  picked in the row of people, **For Ivy…** has two shortcuts: **Sick day** skips what they have
  today (it asks first), and **Hand off to Max** gives Ivy's chores for today and tomorrow to Max.
  Not in Big buttons.
- **Cards or List.** Under the filters, **Cards** and **List** switch how the board looks
  (each person's choice is kept on that device). The list is one line per chore: its status, name,
  who, when and stickers. Tap a name to open its details on the side: its status and its history
  (who did what, and when). **Overdue**, **Today**, **Mine** and **Anyone's** narrow the board,
  and stay in the page's address so you can bookmark a view.
- Outdoor chores show a rain warning when rain is likely (once an admin has set where home is).
- Each chore earns stickers for whoever did it.
- The row of people under the title picks whose chores to see, and shows each person's sticker
  total. Two people with the same first name get a letter of their last name ("Max P.").
- Grown-ups: **+ New chore** to add one (a title, who, and how often; notes, start date and stickers
  are under **More options**), **Manage** to change them, **⋯ → Print** for the fridge.

## The store

Kids spend their stickers here on rewards the grown-ups stock.

- **My wallet** shows how many you have, and **📜 Where they came from**.
- Browse the **Store shelves**. When you have enough, tap **Buy**, then press and hold **Hold to
  buy** for a second (so little thumbs can't buy by accident). Not enough yet? **Save up for this** and watch the progress.
- Some rewards **need an OK** from a grown-up first, and some can only be bought a few times a
  week.
- **My orders** shows what you bought and where it's at. A grown-up will make it happen.
- **Family sheet**: every sticker anyone earns lands here too, for a family goal. Spending never
  takes them back off.

Grown-ups: **Stock the store** to add shelves and rewards. **Orders to make happen** lists what
the kids bought: **Delivered** when it's done, **Refund** to give the stickers back, and for ones
that need an OK, **OK** or **Not now** (with an optional note). Grown-ups can also give a bonus or
take some back from a kid's sticker history. Grown-ups can see everyone's wallets.

## Pet

Everyone can look after a little digital pet. Pick an egg (a Blob, a Birdie or a Critter), give
it a name, then tap the egg a few times to crack it open. (Left alone, it hatches by itself in a
few minutes.)

- **Look after it** with the big buttons: **Feed** (a meal, or a snack, but not too many),
  **Clean** up after it, **Medicine** when it's poorly, and **Say no** when it's making a fuss
  about nothing. The words under the room say how it's feeling; **About** shows its hearts.
- **Play** together: stroke it in the room (it has a favorite spot), play with its toys, or
  pick one of five short games: Snack Catch, Bubble Bath, Hide and Seek, Copy Dance and Fetch
  Far. Games tire it out, so it may want a nap.
- **Room** decorates its room and dresses it up: drag decorations into the room and slide them
  along the wall, the shelf, the windowsill or the floor. The places they fit light up while you
  drag. Drag one out of the room to put it back in the toy box, or use its **Where** list. Toys
  and hats can be dragged into the room too. New toys, decorations and hats come as it grows up
  and the two of you get closer.
- It grows from a baby into a grown-up, and how well it's looked after decides what it grows
  into. Each time it grows (or learns a trick), the room stops for a moment to show you; tap
  **Yay!** to carry on. The **Collection book** keeps every form your pets have grown into, with
  shadows and hints for the ones still to find.
- **Tricks:** as you and your pet get closer, it learns tricks (sit, spin, wave and more). Ask
  for one under **Play**. With good manners it'll do it; otherwise, something sillier.
- It's only awake at certain times of day (a grown-up sets them), so it's fine while
  you're at school or asleep.
- If it's left hungry or poorly for too long, it goes back to Egg Island. Its page stays in
  your **Memory book**, and a new egg is ready straight away.
- **With a keyboard:** every game can be played without a mouse. Snack Catch moves the bowl
  with the left and right arrows, Bubble Bath pops a bubble with Space, Copy Dance uses the
  arrow keys, and Fetch Far throws with Enter. Instead of stroking, **Play** has a button for
  each spot.

The pet has nothing to do with stickers: it never costs any, and nothing in it is bought.

## Meds

Like chores, for medicine: what's due today and what's coming up in the next two weeks. Tick
a dose when it's taken (**Taken** on Home and the Meds page); the undo arrow if it
wasn't. Doses under **Upcoming** can't be ticked ahead: they get their Taken on their day. Doses can be several times a day. Kids can earn
stickers for taking theirs.
The row of people picks whose doses to see, with each person's total, like on Chores.

Grown-ups: **+ New medication** and **Manage**. Stock, prescriber and pharmacy are under **More
options**; people already used as a prescriber or pharmacy come first in those lists.

**Away from home.** On a trip to another time zone, times show home's first and then where you
are ("8:00 AM at home · 2:00 PM here"), so doses stay on their usual schedule. Days stay home's
days. Home says so at the top while you're away.

## Groceries

One shared list for the whole family.

- **Add something…**: type what you need and pick a suggestion, or tap **Scan** to read a
  barcode with the camera (allow the camera when asked; you can always type the number instead).
- Each row has − and + for how many, and who it's for ("For anyone", or a person).
- Tap an item for its details: **How it comes** ("2 lb bag"), **Where we buy it**, its
  section, and what it **Usually** is. Grown-ups see **Last paid**.
- Took something off by mistake? **Undo**.
- **Probably need soon** suggests things you buy regularly.

**Kids** get picture tiles: **Tap what you want!** and it goes on the list. A grown-up can answer
**Not this time**, and you'll get a kind note in your bell.

**At the store: 🛒 Shop.** Big rows, sorted by store and the way you walk the aisles. Tap an item to
put it **In the cart**. The screen stays on while you shop, and ticks work even with no signal:
they catch up when you're back online. When you're done, **Finish trip**: anything you didn't get
stays on the list for next time.

**No signal in the store?** Open Shop once before you go, while you still have signal: your phone
keeps a copy of the list. In a dead spot, Shop opens from that copy with a line saying **The list
as of** a time, **saved on this device**, and your ticks are sent once you're back online. If the
app opens on another page with no signal, it shows **No signal** with a button to **Open the saved
shopping list**. The copy is kept for a week and is removed when someone signs out on that phone.

**After the trip:** grown-ups can **📸 Snap the receipt**. The app reads the prices and matches
them to what you bought, so next time you'll see what you paid. Nobody types prices, and the photo
isn't kept. A line it isn't sure about: type a few letters of the item to find it (this trip's
items come first, then the whole Pantry).

Also here: **🥫 Pantry** (everything the family buys, and tidying up duplicates; one box finds things as you type, and offers **+ Add** for anything new) and **🧾 Past
trips**.

## School

For each kid: **Homework & tests** (tick them off when they're turned in), **Recent grades** and
**Averages**, **Teachers & contacts**, and the **School calendar, next two weeks**. Grown-ups also
see **Forms & to-dos** (things to sign, pay or buy) and who's handling each.

When you add schoolwork, pick its **Type**: homework (the usual), a test, a quiz or a project.
Tests, quizzes and projects are tagged on the list, and the heading says how many tests and quizzes
are coming up this week ("1 test and 2 quizzes this week").

Each piece of homework and each form shows where it stands: **To do**, **Due today**, **Overdue**,
**Turned in**, **Graded 18/20** or **Missing**. When you can change it, the label has a little
arrow: tap it to pick what's next. Kids can move their own
work between **To do** and **Turned in**; only a grown-up marks work **Missing** or clears it.
**Edit** and **Remove** are under each row's **⋯**, and Remove asks first.

**Change things in place.** Tap a piece of homework's words (they have a dotted line and a pencil) to change its title,
class, type or due date right there. Grown-ups also have **Grade it** under the **⋯** for the
score; grading something still to do marks it turned in. Kids can
change their own homework this way too (not once it's missing or graded): the change shows at
once, and grown-ups see **Changed by Ivy** on it, with **Approve** to keep it or **Put back** to
undo it (Put back asks first). The heading says how many changes are waiting, and **Done
recently** opens by itself when one is in there. A grown-up's own edit counts as checking.
Grown-ups with more than one student also get **Everyone**: every student's open homework, tests
and forms in one list, each row saying whose it is. **Overdue**, **This week**, **Tests** and
**Missing** narrow the homework list. **Details & history** under a row's **⋯** opens it on the
side, with what can be changed there and its history; kids see the history of their own work.

Grown-ups can **Select** several homework rows (or forms) and mark them **Turned in**, **To do**
or **Missing** (forms: **Done**) together. For **Forms & to-dos**, the **⋯** has **Edit** (everything about it) and **I've got it** (it's
yours to handle, in one tap).

Kids see their own. Grown-ups set up each kid's school details and add homework. Admins add the
school's calendar under **Calendars**: type the school's website and the app finds its calendar
(or paste the calendar's subscribe link). **Print a fridge card** makes a one-page summary.

## Money (grown-ups)

Money lives in **jars**. Each jar has its own members and nobody else sees it, admins included. You
only get into a jar when you're using the app as yourself, on your own sign-in (not a profile
switched to on someone else's).

Inside a jar:

- **📒 Activity**: every charge, a month at a time. Tap one to set its category ("always for this
  store"), link the order or grocery trip, or make it a **🎁 Surprise** (hidden from chosen people
  until a day you pick). Money back from a store goes in the same category as your last charge
  there, so a return lowers that budget.
- **🎯 Budget**: a monthly budget per category, and saving up for lumpy costs.
- **🔁 Upcoming**: bills and subscriptions, what's due in the next 30 days, and **💸 Safe to
  spend** until payday. It counts regular shops too, like the weekly grocery run, so it stays on
  the careful side; **Not a bill** takes a store out.
- **📈 Trends**: money out month by month, and where it went.
- **💎 Net worth**: what the jar has, minus what it owes.

A jar's Settings connects the bank feed, imports statements and sets your own **Alerts for me**
(bills due, a broken bank feed, over budget, running low). Alerts never include amounts.

**Connecting the bank feed** takes three steps, shown on the page: sign in at SimpleFIN Bridge and
link your banks, make a setup token, paste it. If a bank needs you to sign in again, the page says
so with a link straight to SimpleFIN Bridge. An account that doesn't belong in the jar (say,
Grandma's own card on the same login) can be **left out**: its charges stay in Activity, marked
"left out", but don't count in any total, and nothing new comes from it. **Bring it back**
whenever you like (then tick it again under Safe to spend if it's your checking account).

**Importing a statement file**: download it from the bank or card website (OFX or QFX if it
offers one, else CSV), choose it under **Import a statement**, and press **Check it**. For a CSV,
the app shows what it understood on a few rows: check the dates, the words and whether each
amount is money in or out, fix any dropdown that's off, then **Import**. Got it wrong? **Remove
this import** takes the whole file back out.

**One Teams chat for the jar**: instead of a message to each of you, a jar can send its alerts
once to a Teams group chat. In the jar's Settings, **Choose a Teams chat** lists your own group
chats; pick one with everyone in the jar. The family's Teams app joins the chat to post there; it
can't read what anyone writes. Someone from outside the family's Microsoft 365 is marked, since
they'd see the alerts too.

**🗄️ Keep a copy** on the Money page downloads your own jars (it asks you to sign in again first).

## Cars and the Garage (grown-ups)

Each car's maintenance by miles and/or date (oil changes, tires, registration…), with the service
history and costs, fill-ups and miles per gallon, and recall alerts. **+ New vehicle**, then **+ Add
item** or **Start from common items**. Logging a service resets its countdown. A new item shows
**Not logged yet** until you log when it was last done (a guess is fine); it isn't counted as
overdue until then.

**The VIN** (optional) is on the driver's door frame, the corner of the windshield, or the
insurance card. Type it, or **Scan the VIN** with the phone's camera, then **Look it up**: the make,
model and year fill in (change any of them if you like). A car with a VIN gets **Check this VIN at
NHTSA** on its recall card, with a button to copy the VIN, to see recalls for that exact car.

The **Garage** keeps count of supplies (oil, filters, wiper blades): link one to a maintenance
item and logging that service counts it down.

## Notifications on your phone

Grown-ups can get alerts on their phone: store orders (admins), money alerts, and changes to your
sign-in.

1. **iPhone or iPad:** first add the app to your Home Screen and sign in there (see [Getting
   in](#add-it-to-your-home-screen)). Android: Chrome works as it is.
2. Open **Settings → My notifications**, and under **This phone** tap **Turn on alerts on this
   phone**.
3. Allow notifications when the phone asks.

**My phones** lists your phones; **Remove** stops one. If you blocked notifications by mistake,
allow them in the phone's settings and reload the page.

**In Teams** (grown-ups who sign in with Microsoft): alerts an admin switched on for you arrive as
messages from the family's Teams app, named like the app, under **Chat** in Teams. It only sends;
there's nothing to reply to.

Kids get notes in the bell (orders, "Not this time"), not on their phone.

## Your own settings

**Settings** opens a page of tiles. **Just for you** changes things only for you:

- **My notifications**: phone alerts and which notices you get.
- **My passkeys**: add a spare on another device, rename or remove one. Adding one asks you to
  sign in again first (it's a new way in).
- **Lock my tile**: a PIN on your own tile, so kids on a shared tablet can't open your profile
  (or the money).
- **Calendar on Home** (Microsoft): which of your Outlook calendars Home shows.

Grown-ups also see **Data & backups**: download any section as a spreadsheet for Excel.

Admins see more: the household, sign-ins and invites, Make it yours, apps and alerts.

## For kids

- **Getting in:** a grown-up opens the app. Tap your sticker and type your PIN. Keep your PIN
  secret!
- **Your chores:** tap **Done!** on each one when it's done. Grab the **Anyone** chores to earn
  extra.
- **Your stickers:** see them in **My wallet**. Save up, then **Buy** and **Hold to buy** in the
  store. Some
  things need a grown-up's OK.
- **Groceries:** want something? **Tap what you want!**
- **Meds and school:** your doses and homework are on Home too.
- **Done for now?** Tap your name at the top, then **Switch person**, so the next person uses

## What if…

**I lost my phone.** Tell an admin straight away. They can stop your passkeys everywhere and give
you a new link, good for an hour, to make a new one. They can also stop alerts going to the lost
phone. If you sign in with Microsoft, also tell whoever looks after the family's Microsoft
accounts.

**I forgot my PIN.** Kids: ask an admin to set a new one (Settings → Household). Grown-ups who
locked their own tile: sign out and back in, and your tile opens without the PIN once, right
after; then change it in Settings → Lock my tile. After five wrong tries the tile waits a while
before you can try again.

**It asked me to sign in again.** Normal: the app asks every 30 days, and Cloudflare about once a
month. Some things, like backups, inviting someone, adding a passkey or changing your PIN, ask
you to **Sign in again** if you haven't in the last few minutes.

**It says "That account isn't set up here."** Ask an admin for an invite.

**An app is missing.** It may be switched off for the family, or it's for grown-ups only.

**A page says the app is "turned off for your family".** An admin switched that app off; nothing
in it was deleted.

**A banner says "Emergency local access is on".** Cloudflare is having trouble and an admin opened
the emergency way in at home. Everything works as usual; it goes away when things are back to
normal.

**Times come in pairs, "at home" and "here".** Your phone is in a different time zone from home.
Home's time is first, then the time where you are.

**It says "No signal".** The page needs a connection. If you opened Shop recently, **Open the saved
shopping list** still works; otherwise **Try again** once you have signal.

**I see "Forbidden" or a Cloudflare page instead of the app.** Close it and open the app again.
Still stuck? Tell an admin.

**Something looks wrong.** A number that's off, something missing, a page that won't load: tell an
admin, ideally with what you tapped and a screenshot. Don't worry about breaking anything.
